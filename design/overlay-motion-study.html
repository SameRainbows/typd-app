Those eight facts fully determine the technique: plain DOM elements whose height is written every frame from an analyser's frequency bins. No stored asset, no declarative timeline. Everything above is a from-scratch implementation of that technique — the geometry and response curves are my own calls, which is what the sliders are for.

What I could not verify without looking at the running app: exact bar count, bar width and gap, pill dimensions, corner radius, and whether the frequency mapping mirrors around center. Defaults here are my best read. Put this side by side with the real overlay and the deck will close the gap in about a minute.
