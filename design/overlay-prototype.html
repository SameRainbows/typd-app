Typd — motion study 05

130 by 36. Two circles and eleven bars.

A dictation overlay that asks to be looked at has already failed. This one sits low, stays compact, and keeps a floor under the meter so a silent moment reads as listening rather than as broken. Nothing reports how long you have been talking, because nobody has ever needed to know.

How tall is Mount Everest?
8,849 m (29,032 ft) above sea level — the figure jointly agreed by China and Nepal in 2020.

Eleven bars with a floor

Fourteen hairlines collapsed into a dotted line the moment you stopped talking. Fewer, thicker, and with a minimum height in the geometry rather than the amplitude — silence now breathes instead of flatlining.

The commit circle is themed apart

On ivory, hue is what makes it read as primary. On charcoal a saturated disc is the loudest thing on screen, so dark trades hue for luminance — the pill stays black, grey and ivory, and mode is carried by the label above it.

Cancel is filled, but quiet

Enough of a chip to read as a button, a step down in contrast from the commit circle so the two never compete. It only turns red once the cursor is actually on it.
