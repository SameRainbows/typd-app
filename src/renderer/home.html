Under 18? Ask a parent before you share.

For each dictation: the audio and the text Typd typed (for a revision, only the audio of your spoken instruction), the program you dictated into (e.g. “Discord”), the model and its version, timings, and your OS and Typd version, with a random ID for this install.

Never shared: what’s on your screen, text you had selected, window titles, or anything when you’re not dictating.

It’s used to test new versions of the model and to train it. It’s stored privately, never published, and never sold or given to anyone. Avoid dictating passwords, bank details or anything you wouldn’t want stored.
